Nicknamed the Yankee Dollar by her crew · the only intact ship of 130

She served in the last weeks of the war, and then she served under two more flags for more than fifty years. She came home in 2007, and she outlived every other ship of her kind.

Service in 1945
She arrived at Okinawa on June 18, 1945, and stood radar-picket duty from July 10. After the war she did mine-destruction work along the China coast and rivers.
Three navies
The United States, then Japan as Himawari from 1953, then Thailand as Nakha from 1966.
Home
She returned to the United States in 2007.
A national honor
She was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2015.
Today
She entered the Suisun Bay Reserve Fleet at Benicia, California, on March 30, 2026. She is the only intact ship of her class.
