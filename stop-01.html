Welcome to LCS-102.

This tour brings you the history and stories of this ship, and the fleet it was part of.

During World War II, in the Pacific War, victory came one beach at a time, and every beach had to be taken from an enemy dug into the sand. The big battleships and aircraft carriers made the headlines, but the last stretch of water belonged to a fleet of small gunboats.

They moved in close to the landing beaches and fired to protect the troops going ashore. That job is called close-in fire support, which means firing from near the beach to protect troops as they land.

Sailors called these gunboats the Mighty Midgets. One hundred thirty were built, and about 10,000 sailors served on them. This is the story of that fleet, and of the one ship that is still intact today.
