The war ended in August 1945, and LCS-102 stayed on with the occupation force. She helped destroy mines along the coast of China and up its rivers.

In 1953 she was transferred to Japan, which renamed her Himawari. In 1966 she was transferred to Thailand, which renamed her Nakha. She returned to the United States in 2007.

In 2015 she was listed on the National Register of Historic Places. On March 30, 2026, she entered the Suisun Bay Reserve Fleet at Benicia, California. Of the 130 ships built, she is the only one still intact.
