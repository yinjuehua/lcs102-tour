A ship is only steel and paint, and what made this one worth saving was the people who served aboard ships like her. About 10,000 sailors served on these 130 ships, and time has taken almost all of them.

Some of the men who served these ships never came home. We are working to save the last ship and to build this museum, so that their service is seen, taught, and honored.

The tour closes with Honoring Our Heroes, and then Additional Ways to Explore.
