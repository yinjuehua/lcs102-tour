A ship is only steel and paint, and what made this one worth saving was the people who served aboard ships like her. About 10,000 sailors served on these 130 ships, and time has taken almost all of them.

The last intact ship
USS LCS-102

One of 130 built. She returned to the United States in 2007, and she is the only intact ship of her class left in the world.

The last known sailor
Eddie Desmond

One of about 10,000 who served. He was the cook on LCS-128, a sister ship of LCS-102, and his battle station was a 20-millimeter gun. As of September 2, 2026, available public records identify Eddie Desmond as the last known surviving LCS sailor.

Some of the men who served these ships never came home. We are working to save the last ship and to build this museum, so that their service is seen, taught, and honored.
