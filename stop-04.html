On February 17, 1945, LCS-102 was commissioned, which means she was formally placed into active Navy service. Her crew came aboard, and steel and sailors became one ship.

A new ship then ran a shakedown, the first set of training trips that test the ship and train the crew. The crew drilled until they and the ship worked as one.

Then she sailed for the Pacific, the largest ocean on Earth, crossed by a ship only 158 feet long.
