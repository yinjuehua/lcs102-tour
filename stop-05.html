The day LCS-102 was commissioned, sister ships were already at war.

On February 16, 1945, at Mariveles in the Philippines, Japanese suicide boats attacked the gunboats there. A suicide boat was a small, fast boat packed with explosives, and its pilot drove it straight into a ship. Three sister ships were sunk that night: LCS-7, LCS-26, and LCS-49.

Days later, other sister ships served at the Iwo Jima landings.

While LCS-102 wasn’t part of the historic battles of her sister ships, she would go on to outlive every one of the other 130.
